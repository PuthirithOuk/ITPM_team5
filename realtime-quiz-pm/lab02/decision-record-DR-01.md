# DR-01 · Open-day demo scope, week 10
Status: approved 2026-10-24 · Requested by: Head of IT (sponsor)

## Context
At the end of week 4 the sponsor asked that all Should features (F09 to F13, 110 h) be demonstrable at an open day in week 10, with the week-14 pilot unchanged.
Fixed facts: C-01 pilot with 50 students in week 14 (date unchanged); C-02 capacity 40 h/week, so 240 h for weeks 5 to 10; C-03 cash cap 200 USD, no paid staff; C-06 MVP demo of 8 of 8 Must features in week 7.
Data: Must remaining 152 h. Needed by week 10 for the sponsor's list: 262 h; shortfall 22 h, or 78 h (about 2 weeks) with the 56 h contingency kept.

## Options considered
| Option | Time | Cost | Quality |
|--------|------|------|---------|
| 0 All Should by week 10 | +2 weeks of work in a 6-week window | 78 h over, or 936 USD if bought (breaks C-03) | Not fundable |
| 1 Must + F09 (20 h) + F10 (30 h) by week 10 = 202 h | 0 weeks | 0 USD, 38 h slack (18 h under the 56 h contingency) | Real analytics and responsive design at the open day; F11, F12, F13 come at the pilot |
| 2 Demo build with mocked analytics (8 h) | 0 weeks | 8 h from contingency (56 -> 48 h), 0 USD | Fake data at the open day; full Should scope at week 14 as planned |

## Decision
Option 1. F09 and F10 move to a new "Open day demo" milestone in week 10. F11, F12 and F13 stay at the week-14 pilot. No Could work before week 11.

## Consequences
- New milestone "Open day demo" in week 10 in the schedule baseline, to be updated by week 5.
- New Won't items for the open day (not for the pilot): F11 export, F12 notifications, F13 media, and all Could features F14 to F16.
- Slack in weeks 5 to 10 is 38 h, 18 h under the contingency policy. Risk R-10 "demo on unfinished analytics" is added, owner PM, review at week 7.
- Scope baseline for the pilot is unchanged: 8 Must, 4 Should, Could in the order of Task 2.
