| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Capacity W5-W10 | 6 x 40 = 240 h |
| Must remaining (300 h, 148 h done) | 152 h |
| Should total, sponsor's list F09-F13 (plan classes) | 110 h |

## Script
**Sponsor:** I need every Should feature demonstrable at the open day in week 10. It is mostly small stuff. *(tactic 1: "it is a small change")*
**PM:** I want the open day to work. Those five features add up to 110 h, which is 2.75 weeks of the whole team. With 152 h of Must work left, we need 262 h and have 240 h between week 5 and week 10.
**Sponsor:** So you are 22 hours short, that is nothing. Work a bit harder and you will get there. *(tactic 2: "work a bit harder")*
**PM:** 22 h is real, but it assumes we spend none of the 56 h contingency. Keeping contingency, the gap is 78 h. Closing it means each student goes from 10 to 13.25 h/week, 32.5 % more, for six weeks, and the 10 h/week is the commitment each student made.
**Sponsor:** The other student teams manage this kind of thing. *(tactic 3: "other teams manage")*
**PM:** I do not have their numbers, and I can only plan with ours: 18 and 21 points in the first two sprints, 19.5 on average. What I can give you is our hours: 240 available, 262 requested.
**Sponsor:** Fine, then just start on the Should features now and we will decide later what is really needed. *(tactic 4: "decide later")*
**PM:** Starting now is possible, and the date for the decision matters. Each week without a choice uses 40 h of the 240 h window. If we decide by the end of week 5, all of the options I will show you stay open; after week 7, three weeks remain, 120 h, and the Should list no longer fits at all.
**Sponsor:** The point is the open day. Someone from the faculty will see this.
**PM:** Then let us protect what they will see. Must work stays first: 152 h, so 88 h are left in the window for demo features.
**Sponsor:** 88 hours is not enough for five features.
**PM:** It is enough for two of the five: Responsive design is 20 h and Analytics is 30 h, so 50 h, and 38 h remain.
**Sponsor:** Why those two?
**PM:** Responsive design makes the demo work on any phone in the audience, and analytics is the screen that looks different from other quiz tools. Export is 24 h and notifications 12 h; neither shows on a stage.
**Sponsor:** And the other three?
**PM:** They stay in the plan for the pilot in week 14, as before, so the pilot scope does not change: 24 + 12 + 24 = 60 h in weeks 11 to 14, within the capacity of 160 h for those weeks.
**PM:** Here are two ways to give you a strong open day. Option 1: Must complete plus Responsive (20 h) and Analytics (30 h) by week 10 = 202 h, which keeps 38 h of slack and displaces nothing but the order of export, notifications and media, which move to the pilot phase. Option 2: a demo build with mocked analytics (8 h) at week 10 and the full Should scope at week 14 as planned, which adds 8 h and takes them from the contingency, 56 down to 48 h, and displaces no feature.
**Sponsor:** I take option 1. The faculty will see real analytics.
**PM:** Good. I will record this as DR-01 today and update the baseline by week 5. Risk R-10, "demo on unfinished analytics", goes into the register with the baseline, and no Could work starts before week 11.

Turns: 19. Tactics used: 1 small change, 2 work a bit harder, 3 other teams manage, 4 decide later. Every PM turn carries a number from the data sheet.
